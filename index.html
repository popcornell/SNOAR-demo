SNOAR: Self-training with Noise filtering, Observation adding, and Remixing for Speech Denoising Without Clean References

WHAM!CHiME-3 real

Unsupervised denoisers such as MixIT and noisy-target training learn directly from noisy recordings but remain well below supervised training. Teacher–student self-training, as in RemixIT, can improve them without clean references, but the student only learns the teacher's estimates, so teacher errors, in particular over-suppressed speech, are propagated rather than corrected. We propose SNOAR (self-training with noise filtering, observation adding and remixing), which corrects the teacher's estimates before using them as self-supervision: differential noise filtering (DNF) removes the noise the teacher leaks, and adding back part of the noisy observation restores suppressed speech and attenuates artifacts. On WHAM!, SNOAR from a MixIT teacher comes within 0.43 dB SI-SDR of a supervised topline and outperforms RemixIT on every metric. On real CHiME-3 recordings it improves all three teachers it starts from, whereas RemixIT falls below each of them.
